How it works

  1. Reserve. Pick an offer and give your ZP address. The offer is held for you (about 80 minutes up to 0.01 BTC, 2 hours up to 0.1 BTC, 4 hours above); nobody else can take it and the seller cannot cancel it. Pay in the first 30, 45 or 60 minutes.
  2. Pay. Send the exact amount to the Bitcoin address shown. It is the seller's own address, made for your reservation only.
  3. Receive. Once the payment has enough confirmations (2 up to 0.01 BTC, 3 up to 0.1, 4 up to 1, 6 above), the proof is sent for you and the contract releases the ZP to your address. You do not need to do anything.

Selling ZP: an offer locks your ZP in the contract with your price and a few Bitcoin addresses from your wallet (one per reservation). Sellers make and take back offers in the ZP Wallet, on its Markets screen. Testnet: ZP coins have no value.

Is this real money?

No. Base Sepolia and the ZP testnet are test networks; their USDC, ETH and ZP have no value. Never use a wallet or key that holds real funds here.

Who holds the USDC?

One operator key holds the reserves and is the only key that can issue or destroy zUSDC. Fine for a prototype, not for real money: before any real use the plan is multi-signature custody decided by a community vote, limits, a pause switch and an independent audit.

I deposited before linking

Nothing is lost. The deposit waits and is issued as soon as the sending wallet is linked.

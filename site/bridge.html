Use the bridge

You need a browser wallet such as MetaMask with a little test ETH and test USDC on Base Sepolia (free: Circle faucet for USDC), and a ZP testnet address (ZP Wallet, test ZP from the faucet).

  1. Connect your EVM wallet

    The page asks your wallet to switch to Base Sepolia. Nothing is sent in this step.

  2. Link it to your ZP address

    You sign a message (no fee) that tells the bridge where to send your zUSDC. Do this once per EVM address.

  3. Deposit USDC

    Send USDC to the bridge. After 12 confirmations on Base Sepolia (about half a minute) the same amount of zUSDC is issued to your linked ZP address.

  4. Withdraw back to USDC

    Send zUSDC from your linked ZP address to the bridge's ZP address (below), then paste that transaction here. The bridge destroys the zUSDC and pays USDC to your EVM address. In the ZP Wallet: Send, choose zUSDC, paste the bridge's ZP address; the transaction id shows when it is sent.

Good to know

Is this real money?

No. Base Sepolia and the ZP testnet are test networks; their USDC, ETH and ZP have no value. Never use a wallet or key that holds real funds here.

Who holds the USDC?

One operator key holds the reserves and is the only key that can issue or destroy zUSDC. That is fine for a prototype and not for real money: before any real use the plan is multi-signature custody decided by a community vote, limits, a pause switch and an independent audit.

I deposited before linking

Nothing is lost. The deposit waits and is issued as soon as the sending EVM address is linked (step 2).

How can I check that zUSDC is backed?

The two numbers at the top: USDC the bridge's address holds on Base Sepolia (read from the chain, open it on BaseScan below) and zUSDC issued minus destroyed. The first must be at least the second.
